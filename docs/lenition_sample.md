## Stems attested nowhere, and short

Rows where the stem occurs neither in `data/GA` nor as a MoirfEolas row, and is shorter than 4 characters. This is where a wrong demutation is both most likely and hardest to spot, because a short stem can look like a word without being one.

10 of 6,803 rows.

| word | stem | consonant |
|------|------|:---------:|
| Chuí | Cuí | C |
| Phós | Pós | P |

| dhóú | dóú | d |
| ghor | gor | g |
| mhea | mea | m |
| phot | pot | p |
| thát | tát | t |

`bhur` is the clearest false positive in the whole export: it is the possessive pronoun, not a lenited `bur`. It is the kind of thing a purely formal analyser cannot see, and the reason a lenition column wants a pass by eye over the short unattested stems before it is trusted.

For context, 2,557 rows of 6,803 (37.6%) have a stem attested in neither source at any length. Most of those are long inflected forms whose lemma is in the corpus but whose exact form is not, so the flag is weak evidence on its own; combined with a short stem it is much stronger.
