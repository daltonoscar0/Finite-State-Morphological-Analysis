## 7. Lenition: what splitting it costs today

Lenition is an infix, so no boundary split can isolate it. `hybrid_splinter` moves the marker out and leaves the stem contiguous, so its tokens no longer concatenate back to the surface.

## 9. Three things in her code, raised not patched

| issue | count |
|-------|------:|
| words with an empty first token, SP-ULM 8k baseline | 3,605 |
| the same, SP-ULM 8k hybrid | 4,350 |

| eclipsed capitalised vowels the gold misses | 60 |

`remove_specials` strips a leading `▁` unconditionally, so a standalone `▁` piece becomes an empty first token that then fails every position-anchored test. It affects her published SentencePiece rows exactly as much as it affects mine, which is why it is raised rather than patched, and why the Unigram comparison is the clean one.

The missed capitals are all eclipsis of a capitalised vowel written without a hyphen: `nAcht`, `nAchtanna`, `nAfracánach`, `nAglachlach`, `nAirmhitear`, `nAirí`. `SPECIAL_PREFIXES` maps bare `n` to `d g D G` only. The spurious `t` rows are the mirror: `téad`, `tuartha`, `tiarratas`, `tiompar` read as `t` plus a vowel. Both look like one case condition.
